  METHODOLOGICAL CAVEATS:
  1. Pseudo-replication: jeff_ch has only ~7 distinct values inherited
     from parent classes. Effective df is closer to 7 than to n.
     Per-chapter descriptives above are a more reliable primary source.
  2. Geographic hierarchy ambiguity: ch.29 Geography (G class, corrected
     to Reason) subdivides into General/Europe/Asia/Africa/America — five
     sub-entries each mapping to different LCC subclasses. The chapter
     count at the subclass level is inherently ambiguous for G subclasses.
  3. The log scale compresses extreme outliers (HD, HC) that dominated
     the raw-scale correlation. Spearman is robust to both.
  4. Primary test of hierarchy persistence remains Test D (ABSENT vs
     classified inflection timing), which uses subclass-level status.

TEST D [All subclasses]  ABSENT vs. classified — inflection timing (t0)
H0: inflection year distributions are equal
H1: ABSENT subclasses inflect later (suppression predicts delayed takeoff)

Dependent variable: normalized sigmoid inflection year t0 (year each
subclass reached 50% of its 2020 cumulative total).
Restricted to reliable sigmoid fits (r2>=0.85, n=198).

TWO VERSIONS reported:
  Version A: RENAMED excluded from classified (matches original Test D)
  Version B: RENAMED included in classified (AC, AE, AG are pre-LCC
             encyclopedic collections with Jefferson-era antecedents)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  ABSENT      n=117  mean=1984.8  median=1986.6  IQR=[1982, 1990]
  MERGED+DIRECT n= 78  mean=1983.6  median=1984.0  IQR=[1981, 1987]
  RENAMED     n=  3  mean=1952.8  median=1974.3  (AC=1902.7, AE=1974.3, AG=1981.3)

  Shapiro-Wilk ABSENT        : W=0.605, p=0.0000 (NON-NORMAL)
  Shapiro-Wilk Classified    : W=0.545, p=0.0000 (NON-NORMAL)

  Version A (RENAMED excluded, n_classified=78):
    Gap: +1.2 years (ABSENT later)
    Gap as % of 200-yr study period: 0.6%
    Mann-Whitney U=5552.5,  p=0.0104
    Rank-biserial r=-0.217  (small)
    RESULT: SIGNIFICANT (p=0.0104)
    [Original Test D reported p=0.0044 — matches this version]

  Version B (RENAMED included, n_classified=81):
    Gap: +2.3 years (ABSENT later)
    Gap as % of 200-yr study period: 1.2%
    Mann-Whitney U=5868.5,  p=0.0044
    Rank-biserial r=-0.238  (small)
    RESULT: SIGNIFICANT (p=0.0044)

  INTERPRETATION:
  RENAMED subclasses (AC=1902.7, AE=1974.3, AG=1981.3) are old
  encyclopedic collections cataloged early in the LCC era. Including
  them pulls the classified mean earlier, widening the gap slightly.
  Both versions produce the same substantive conclusion.

  *** SIGNIFICANT in both versions — p < 0.05 ***
  The gap (1.2 yrs, Version A) is statistically detectable but
  trivially small: 0.6% of the 200-year study period.
  Both groups' IQRs overlap substantially in the 1980-1990 MARC window.
  A ~2-year gap occurring 155 years after Jefferson cannot be
  attributed to his 1815 framework. The mechanical explanation
  (classified subclasses had more pre-MARC holdings to retroactively
  catalog in 1968) is more parsimonious than hierarchy persistence.

TEST E [Full catalog]  Classified vs. ABSENT — inflection timing
                       across all parent classes
H0: inflection year distributions equal for classified and absent subclasses
H1: classified subclasses inflect earlier across the whole catalog

Original Test E asked this only within the P class (17 subclasses).
This version runs the same question across all 198 reliable subclasses,
then also reports the P-class comparison for direct comparison.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  FULL CATALOG (n=198 reliable fits):
    Classified (MERGED+DIRECT) n= 78  mean=1983.6  median=1984.0  range=[1966, 1995]
    ABSENT                     n=117  mean=1984.8  median=1986.6  range=[1883, 2005]

  Gap: +1.2 years (ABSENT inflects later)
  Gap as % of 200-yr study period: 0.6%
  Mann-Whitney U=3573.5,  p=0.0052 [one-sided, classified < absent]
  Rank-biserial r=+0.217  (small)
  RESULT: SIGNIFICANT (p=0.0052)

  Classified subclasses inflect earlier across the full catalog.
  However, interpret direction carefully:
  - Classified subclasses (MERGED/DIRECT) tend to be older, larger
    collections with more pre-MARC holdings to retroactively
    catalog when machine-readable cataloging began in 1968.
  - This gives them a mechanical head start unrelated to Jefferson's
    hierarchy. The gap size and IQR overlap are the key diagnostics.

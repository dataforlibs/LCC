REBUTTAL TO: "JEFFERSON'S CLASSIFICATION SUPPRESSED DEMOCRATIC/SOCIAL KNOWLEDGE DOMAINS"
Finding: The share of "democratic" classes (H Social Sciences, E American History, F Local History, J Political Science, G Geography) in total LC holdings doubled from ~11% (1815) to ~21% (2020), while Jefferson-heavy classes (K Law, P Language & Lit, B Philosophy) fell from ~44% to ~34%.
Implication: If the classification system encoded a hierarchy that privileged Jeffersonian elite knowledge, it failed completely. Over two centuries the system amplified, not suppressed, democratic subject domains.
H+E+F+J+G = Social Sciences, American History, Local History, Political Science, Geography  ·  K+P+B = Law, Language & Lit, Philosophy
